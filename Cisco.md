# Summary: Cisco Cybersecurity Notes

## 1.1.3 Your Online Identity

**Core idea:** Your username shouldn't expose personal information or make you easier to track or hack.

**Don't:**
- Use your full name, address, or phone number
- Use your email username
- Reuse the same username and password combination (especially on financial accounts)
- Pick an odd username and reuse it everywhere (makes you trackable)
- Choose a username that hints at your password (number/letter series, first half of a phrase like "knock-knock," or your department like "IT")

**Do:**
- Choose a username that fits the account type (business, social, personal)

**Example:**

| Bad | Why | Better |
|---|---|---|
| `JohnSmith_Bangkok88` | Full name and location | `BlueKite_Gamer` (social) |
| `john.smith` (same as email) | Matches email username | `J.Smith.Consulting` (business) |
| `starlight123` | Hints at the password | `RiverStone47` (personal), with an unrelated password |

## 1.2.1.2 Internet of Things (IoT) and Big Data

**Core idea:** IoT is a network of physical objects (sensors, software, equipment) connected to the Internet that collect and share data. Cloud and virtualization storage let that data grow exponentially, creating **Big Data**.

**Chain:** IoT devices → data collected and shared → cloud storage expands → exponential data growth → Big Data

**Example:** A smart thermostat, a fitness watch, and traffic sensors all constantly send data. Together they produce huge datasets that businesses analyze.

## 1.2.3 What Do You Think? (Phishing Check)

**Core idea:** Hackers make emails look legitimate to trick you into giving up personal information.

**Check these four things:**
1. **Sender's email domain:** does it match the real company?
2. **URL link:** where does it actually point?
3. **Language, spelling, and grammar:** are there errors?
4. **Graphics:** are logos or images off or low quality?

**Example:** An email from `support@paypa1-security.com` (the "l" is a "1") with the message "Your acount is locked, click here immediately" fails checks 1, 2, and 3.
